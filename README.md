## 🚀 About Me

My interest in electrical engineering started at an early age. Growing up around the electrical power industry sparked my curiosity about how machines, electrical systems, and electronic devices work. By the time I was eight, I was already taking apart electronic toys simply to understand what was inside.

That curiosity eventually developed into a career focused on **electrical engineering, industrial automation, control systems, and energy technologies**.

During my undergraduate studies, I worked with **Arduino, embedded systems, robotics, and Raspberry Pi**, later developing a Python-based multi-factor security system as my final project. As my engineering experience expanded into industrial environments, I became increasingly interested in **PLCs, instrumentation, motor control, industrial communication, HMI development, and process automation**.

Today, my work and personal projects combine **PLC programming, industrial automation, control systems, MATLAB/Simulink, Python, instrumentation, and energy systems** to design practical and reliable engineering solutions.

Outside engineering, I enjoy traveling, biking, exploring new technologies, and occasionally gaming.
